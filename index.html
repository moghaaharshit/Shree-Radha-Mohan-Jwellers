We do not collect your precise location, your contact list, your SMS, your call logs, your photos or files, your microphone or camera, or any advertising identifier.

  • To receive and respond to your enquiries, appointment requests and plan requests.
  • To send you service notifications (for example, a reply to your request or an update you asked for).
  • To keep the App secure, prevent misuse and fix technical problems.
  • To meet our legal and accounting obligations.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data. We do not use your data for third-party advertising, and the App does not contain third-party ad networks.

5. How your data is stored and protected

Your data is stored in the following trusted service providers, used only to run the App:

ProviderPurposeData involved

Some of these providers may process data on servers located outside India. Where that happens, we rely on the provider's contractual and security safeguards for international transfers.

6. Who we share data with

We share personal data only in these limited situations:

We never share your data with advertisers, data brokers or unrelated third parties.

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile and request records are deleted from our live systems, and residual backups are purged within 30 days, unless a longer period is required by law (for example, tax and accounting records).

The App is intended for users aged 18 years and above. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

The App does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, files, location or phone state.

We use industry-standard measures: encrypted transport, Firebase Authentication, restricted database access rules, and limited staff access.
