Delete Your Account

Shree Radha Mohan Jewellers

Android App · com.shreeradhamohan.jewellers

You can delete your Shree Radha Mohan Jewellers account and the personal data linked to it at any time. There are two ways to do it: inside the app (fastest), or by sending us an email request.

Both options are free, and neither requires you to give a reason.

App: Shree Radha Mohan Jewellers Developer: Shree Radha Mohan Jewellers Last updated: 24 November 2026
1

Delete it inside the app

Instant

This is the quickest way and it happens immediately, without waiting for a reply from us.

  1. Open the Shree Radha Mohan Jewellers app and sign in, if you are not already signed in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab (bottom navigation).
  3. Scroll down and tap “Delete account”.
  4. Confirm the prompt. Your account and profile data are deleted right away.
Note: You can only delete the account you are currently signed in with. If you signed up with email and password, keep the password handy in case the app asks you to sign in again before deletion.
2

Send an email request

Within 30 days

If you cannot open the app, have uninstalled it, or cannot sign in, email us and we will delete the account for you. Please write from the same email address you used to sign in to the app, so we can verify the request.

Email your deletion request to
mohanradha209@gmail.com

Subject: Account Deletion Request

Open email app

Please include these details

  • Full name used in the app
  • Registered email address (the one you signed in with)
  • Mobile number saved in your profile
  • Sign-in method — Google, or email & password
  • Optional: your customer ID if you have one

Copy-paste template

Subject: Account Deletion Request Hello Shree Radha Mohan Jewellers, Please delete my account and the personal data linked to it. Full name: Registered email: Mobile number: Sign-in method (Google / email and password): Thank you.

What happens next

  • We confirm we received your request, usually within 7 working days.
  • We may ask you a quick verification question to make sure the account belongs to you.
  • The account and its data are deleted within 30 days of verification, and you receive a confirmation email.

Good to know

Deleting only some data

You do not have to delete the whole account. Write to us at mohanradha209@gmail.com if you only want to correct your name, change your phone number, or remove your request history. Some of these you can change yourself in the app's Profile screen.

Accounts for children

The app is meant for users aged 18 and above. If an account was created by a child, a parent or guardian can email us and we will delete it promptly.
